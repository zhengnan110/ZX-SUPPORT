WaterSort: Crazy Puzzle Support

Thank you for playing WaterSort: Crazy Puzzle. This page provides gameplay help and contact information.

How to Play

  • Tap the bottle you want to pour from.
  • Tap another bottle to choose the destination.
  • Liquid can be poured when there is enough space and the top colors are compatible.
  • Complete the level by sorting the liquid so that each bottle contains only one color.

Troubleshooting

If you cannot continue a level: restart the current level and try a different sequence of moves.

If the app does not respond: close and reopen the app. If the problem continues, restart your device and make sure iOS is up to date.

When contacting support: include your device model, iOS version, app version, level number, and a short description of the issue.
