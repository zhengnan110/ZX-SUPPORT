Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WaterSort: Crazy Puzzle (the "App") handles information when you use the App.

Information We Collect

The current version of the App does not collect, transmit, store, or share personal data. The App does not require an account or sign-in.

Local Game Data

Game progress and settings may be stored locally on your device so the App can remember your progress. This information is not transmitted to us. You can remove locally stored data by deleting the App.

Advertising and Analytics

The current version of the App does not contain third-party advertising or analytics services.

Tracking

The App does not track users across apps or websites and does not use data for targeted advertising.

Device Permissions

The App does not require access to your camera, microphone, photo library, contacts, or precise location for gameplay.

Children's Privacy

Because the App does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-Party Links

The App does not currently include links to third-party websites or services. If this changes in a future version, the privacy practices of those services will be governed by their own policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features or data practices change. Any updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
