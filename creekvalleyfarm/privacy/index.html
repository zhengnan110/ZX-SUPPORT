Creek Valley Farm Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Creek Valley Farm (the "App"), published by Yongmei Shi, handles information when you use its local gameplay, Apple sign-in, cloud-save, and purchase features.

Local Game Data

The App stores game progress and settings on your device. Core gameplay can be used without signing in. Local data may remain in device backups according to your device settings. Deleting the App or erasing its data may remove local progress.

Apple Sign-In and Cloud Saves

When you use online features, the service processes the stable user identifier supplied by Apple and authentication credentials needed to maintain the session and revoke the App's authorization when an account is deleted. We do not receive your Apple Account password.

Cloud-save features process encrypted game-save data, synchronization versions, and account associations so that progress can be uploaded, retrieved, and synchronized. The service uses an installation-level device identifier and App Attest security information to protect accounts, verify app integrity, and prevent replay or fraudulent requests.

Cloud saves are encrypted before upload and stored as ciphertext. The service uses Cloudflare hosting and database services to operate these online features.

In-App Purchases

Apple handles purchase authorization and payment. We process product identifiers, transaction identifiers, transaction status, and related fulfillment information to grant diamonds, prevent duplicate credits, handle refunds, and investigate purchase issues. We do not receive or store payment-card details.

Technical Service Data and Support

Online requests expose network and request information to the service and its hosting provider. Technical data may include an IP address, request time, error information, and identifiers necessary to operate and secure the service. This information is used for service delivery, security, troubleshooting, and reliability.

If you contact support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to provide. We use that correspondence to respond to your request and resolve the reported issue.

How Information Is Used

  • Authenticate players and maintain optional cloud accounts.
  • Save and synchronize game progress across devices.
  • Verify purchases, grant items, and process purchase-related events.
  • Prevent fraud, replay, and duplicate fulfillment.
  • Provide support and keep online services reliable.

Service Providers, Advertising, and Tracking

Apple provides authentication, app-integrity, and purchase services. Cloudflare provides hosting and database infrastructure. These providers may process information necessary to deliver their services under their own privacy terms.

The current App does not provide third-party advertising or cross-company tracking. We do not sell personal information.

Provider information: Apple Privacy Policy and Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Storage, Retention, and Deletion

Local progress remains on your device until it is changed or removed by you or by the device's storage settings. Cloud account and save information is maintained to provide the online features and is subject to account-deletion requests.

Transaction, security, technical, or support records may be kept as needed to fulfill purchases, handle refunds and disputes, prevent abuse, maintain service reliability, or meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. Retention depends on the type of record and the purpose for which it is processed.

To request cloud account deletion, open Settings > Cloud Save & Apple Sign In > Delete Cloud Account and follow the confirmation steps. This requests deletion of the associated cloud account and saves and revocation of the App's Apple authorization. The confirmation screen explains the effect on the local save. If you cannot use this control, contact us using the email below.

You may also contact us to request information about, correction of, or deletion of data associated with your cloud account. We may need to verify the account before processing a request.

Device Permissions

The current App does not request camera, microphone, photo-library, contacts, or precise-location access. Apple may present its own sign-in and purchase dialogs when you choose those features.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect children's personal information where parental consent is required. If you believe a child has provided personal information without the required consent, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when features, services, or data practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
