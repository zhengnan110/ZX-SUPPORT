Creek Valley Farm Support

Creek Valley Farm is a landscape farming game for iPhone and iPad. Grow crops, care for animals, craft products, plan roads and buildings, and deliver orders across a pixel-art valley.

Getting Started

  1. Launch the game and follow the new-player tutorial.
  2. Plant crops and collect the harvest when they are ready.
  3. Build production facilities, connect them with roads, and assign farm workers.
  4. Turn your harvest into products and complete resident orders to earn coins and experience.
  5. Open Settings > Language to select English, Simplified Chinese, Korean, or Japanese.

Core gameplay does not require signing in. Cloud saves and diamond purchases require an internet connection and Sign in with Apple.

Cloud Saves and Apple Sign-In

Open Settings > Cloud Save & Apple Sign In to use online save features. Sign in with the same Apple Account when accessing your cloud account on another device. Read any synchronization or restore confirmation carefully before replacing your current progress.

Game progress is also stored locally. Deleting the app or erasing its data may remove local progress, so check your cloud-save state before reinstalling.

Diamond Purchases

Diamond packs are consumable virtual game items purchased through Apple's In-App Purchase system. The game offers packs containing 60, 198, 345, 816, 1600, or 4264 total diamonds, including any displayed bonus diamonds. Prices and availability depend on your App Store country or region.

Sign in with Apple in the game before purchasing and wait for the shop to load prices. If a completed purchase has not been credited, keep the purchase details and contact support. Include the product, purchase time, app version, and Apple transaction or order reference if available. Do not send payment-card details or your Apple Account password.

For refunds, use Apple's purchase-support channels. Purchased diamonds that have already been used are not restored as new packs by a standard restore operation.

Troubleshooting

Apple sign-in or cloud saves are unavailable: Check your internet connection and that your device is signed in to an Apple Account. Keep the app in the foreground while retrying. If an integrity-check or server error continues, contact support with the exact message.

The purchase button is disabled or a price is missing: Sign in with Apple in the game, check your connection, and confirm that In-App Purchases are permitted on the device. Wait for the shop to finish loading or reopen it.

A save is missing or different on another device: Confirm that you are using the same Apple Account in the game. Check whether a cloud save exists before choosing a restore action. Avoid deleting the app as your first troubleshooting step.

Production or deliveries are not progressing: Check the required materials, assigned workers, road connections, storage space, and any on-screen messages.

The game closes unexpectedly: Restart the app and device, install the latest available version, and contact support if the issue repeats.

When reporting a problem, include the device model, system version, app version, a short description, and a screenshot if helpful.

Cloud Account Deletion

Open Settings > Cloud Save & Apple Sign In > Delete Cloud Account and follow the confirmation steps. This requests removal of the associated cloud account and save data and revocation of the app's Apple sign-in authorization. Follow the on-screen explanation about any local save remaining on the device.

If you cannot access the app, contact the email below with the subject "Creek Valley Farm account deletion." We may request information needed to identify the account before processing a request.
