Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

This policy describes how Ant Video Editor and its iOS Share extension handle information. The app provides video editing on iPhone and iPad. For privacy questions, contact zhengnanzhengnan@gmail.com.

Projects and Media on Your Device

When you choose to import or create content, the app processes projects, videos, photos, audio, voice recordings, captions, text, stickers, templates, effects, thumbnails, analysis results, and exports. These files and editing settings are stored primarily on your device. Opening the app or its purchase screen does not automatically upload your media to us. Your iOS backup settings may back up locally stored app data.

Face-protection tools analyze selected video locally to create editable overlays and project data. They are not an identity verification service. Review overlays before export because automatic detection can miss or misplace a face.

Accounts and Purchases

The app does not require an app-specific account. The optional Full Unlock purchase is processed by Apple through StoreKit. The app uses verified purchase status to decide whether to add its brand watermark to future video exports. We do not receive your payment card number or Apple Account password through the app. You can restore purchases from the VIP screen.

Permissions

Photos access supports media selection and saving exports. Microphone access is requested when you start voiceover recording. Speech Recognition supports captions you choose to generate. Local Network access supports optional connections you explicitly start, including a paired MCP editing service, web media management, and configured computer-based recognition. You can change these permissions in iOS Settings; basic editing does not require these optional connections.

Optional Speech and Network Services

Speech recognition may run on the device where supported. If you allow network-based Apple recognition, selected audio may be sent to Apple under Apple's terms. If you configure a separate computer or service for recognition, selected audio and task information are sent to that destination under its own practices.

The MCP editing service and web media library are off until you start them. A paired client or browser may access project information, media previews, uploaded files, or exports according to the permissions you grant. An external AI client may send information onward to its own provider. Choose trusted devices and services, limit access, and stop the service or revoke pairing when finished. Sharing or exporting content sends it to the destination you select. The Share extension stores selected items locally until you handle them in the main app.

Diagnostics and Support

The app keeps local settings, operational logs, and diagnostic information. A diagnostic upload occurs only when you choose an address and initiate it; the submitted logs or screenshots may contain device details, file information, or visible personal content. The destination you configure determines who receives and retains that information. Review the contents and use a trusted HTTPS destination where possible.

If you email us for support, we and our email provider receive the contact details and materials you send. We use them to respond to your request and retain them as needed to handle it and meet applicable obligations. You may email us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable requirements. Please do not send passwords, private keys, or unnecessary personal media.

Advertising and Tracking

The current app version does not include in-app advertising or use your editing content for cross-app advertising tracking. If advertising, analytics, or other data practices change, this policy and the app's privacy disclosures will be updated.

Storage and Deletion

Projects and media remain on your device until you manage or remove them. Deleting a project record may leave shared source media, exports, or files you saved elsewhere. Clear Cache removes rebuildable thumbnails; it does not delete all projects, recordings, or exports. Check the app, Files, Photos, paired devices, other apps, and backups for additional copies. Uninstalling the app does not erase files already shared or saved outside it.

Children

Ant Video Editor is a general-purpose creative tool and is not designed specifically for children. Parents and guardians should supervise minors' purchases, permissions, and sharing as appropriate.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when app features, connected services, or data practices change. The current version and effective date will be posted here.
