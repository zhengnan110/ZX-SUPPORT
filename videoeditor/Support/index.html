Ant Video Editor Support

Ant Video Editor lets you create and edit videos on iPhone and iPad. Work with a timeline, text, stickers, audio, effects, covers, and editable example projects.

Getting Started

  1. Choose Create Project on Home, select a layout, and import a video or photo. You can also open an Example Project.
  2. Edit clips and preview your work, then choose Export Video. Save the result in the app, Files, or Photos.
  3. To import selected media from another app, use the iOS Share sheet and choose Ant Video Editor. Return to the main app to choose where to use the imported items.

One-Time Purchase

Existing editing tools are available without a purchase. Free video exports include an ANTVE app-brand watermark. The optional, one-time Full Unlock purchase permanently removes that watermark from future video exports. It does not remove text or logos you added yourself, and it does not alter files already exported or exports in progress.

Open the VIP screen from the gold banner on Home, More > Settings > VIP, or the membership option while exporting. The purchase screen shows Apple's local price. Use Restore Purchases on that screen if you previously bought it with your Apple Account.

Troubleshooting

Export fails: Check free storage, try a shorter project or lower export settings, and make sure the source files are still available.

Watermark still appears after purchase: Restore Purchases, confirm your VIP status, and start a new export. Existing files are not rewritten.

Imported media is missing: Open Ant Video Editor after completing the Share extension and check the import inbox or destination selection.

Captions or face protection need correction: Review recognition results and face overlays before export. Device, language, and lighting can affect automatic analysis.

Permissions and Optional Connections

Photos access is used when you select media or save to Photos. Microphone access is requested when you record a voiceover; Speech Recognition is used for captions you request. Local Network access is used only for optional services you start, such as paired MCP editing, web media management, or a configured computer-based transcription service. You can manage permissions in iOS Settings.

Optional external AI clients and computer transcription services require your own setup. Keep the app in the foreground while using the local-network services, and pair only with devices you trust.
