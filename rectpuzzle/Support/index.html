RectPuzzle Support

RectPuzzle is a relaxing 8×8 logic puzzle for iPhone and iPad. Place all eight colorful rectangles on the board without covering the stone blockers, complete 88 handcrafted levels, and improve your best times and star ratings.

Getting Started

  1. Select a level from the level-selection screen.
  2. Drag a colored rectangle onto the 8×8 board.
  3. Double-tap a rectangle to rotate it.
  4. Place every rectangle without covering a stone blocker to complete the level.
  5. If you get stuck, use a hint to highlight a legal next move.

Coins, Purchases, and Rewarded Ads

Coins can be earned from the daily gift or optional rewarded ads and can also be purchased as consumable coin packs through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Coins are virtual game items with no cash value and cannot be transferred or redeemed.

Troubleshooting

A piece will not fit: Make sure every occupied square is inside the board and does not overlap a stone blocker or another piece. Double-tap the piece to try the other orientation.

A purchase is unavailable: Check your internet connection and confirm that In-App Purchases are allowed for your Apple Account. Restart the App and try again. Consumable coin packs cannot be restored after they have been used.

A rewarded ad is unavailable: Ads depend on internet access, regional consent, and ad availability. A reward is granted only after an eligible video is completed, with a maximum of three rewarded ads per local calendar day.

Progress or coins are missing: Progress, settings, records, and the coin balance are stored locally on the device. Deleting the App or erasing its data may permanently remove them.

If the problem continues, include your device model, iOS version, App version, and a brief description when contacting support.
