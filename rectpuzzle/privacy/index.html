Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RectPuzzle (the "App") handles information when you use the App.

Information Stored on Your Device

Game progress, settings, coin balance, challenge times, star ratings, local leaderboard records, and information used to prevent duplicate in-app rewards are stored locally on your device. RectPuzzle does not operate a cloud-save service. This local information remains on the device until you erase the App's data or delete the App.

Accounts and Sign-In

RectPuzzle does not require an account or sign-in. The App does not ask you to provide a name, email address, password, or profile.

Device Permissions

The current version does not request access to your camera, microphone, photo library, contacts, health data, or precise location.

Advertising and Consent

The App uses Google Mobile Ads to offer optional rewarded advertisements and Google User Messaging Platform to manage privacy choices where required. Depending on your region, consent choices, device settings, and Google's services, Google may process information such as IP-derived approximate location, device identifiers, ad views, product interactions, advertising data, crash information, and performance information for ad delivery, fraud prevention, reporting, analytics, and App functionality.

The current version does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission. It disables the Google publisher first-party identifier and Google Mobile Ads SDK crash reporting. When required, a consent form is shown before ads are requested, and a Privacy Options entry is available in the App's Settings screen when Google requires it.

For more information about Google's data practices, visit the Google Privacy Policy.

In-App Purchases

Consumable coin-pack purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple handles your payment information; RectPuzzle does not receive your card or bank details. Purchase transaction information may be used to verify a purchase and prevent duplicate delivery. Apple's handling of information is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Data Retention and Deletion

You can remove locally stored game information by deleting the App from your device. Deleting the App may permanently remove progress and unspent coins. Information processed by Apple or Google is retained and deleted according to their respective policies and available account or privacy controls.

Children's Privacy

RectPuzzle does not knowingly collect names, email addresses, or other direct contact information from children. Rewarded ads and consent behavior follow the serving and privacy configuration applied through Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform. If you believe a child has provided personal information in connection with the App, contact us so we can review the request.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, third-party services, or data practices change. Any updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
