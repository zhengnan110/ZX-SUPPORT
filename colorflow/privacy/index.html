Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Colorflow Colony (the "App") handles information when you use the App.

Information Stored on Your Device

Game progress, unlocked artwork, settings, best times, and local rankings are processed and stored locally on your device. The App does not require an account or sign-in, and this gameplay information is not uploaded to a Colorflow Colony server.

Photo Library

The App may request permission to add an image to Photos when you choose to save completed artwork, a bead-art preview, or a numbered craft pattern. This permission is used only for a save action that you initiate.

The App does not need to read or scan your existing photo library for normal gameplay. If you deny permission, you can continue playing, but the selected image cannot be saved to Photos.

Advertising and Analytics

Production advertising and rewarded-ad requests are disabled in the current submitted version. The current version does not actively use analytics, crash-reporting, account, purchase, or cloud-sync services.

The App package may include software components intended to support advertising in a future version, but those advertising functions are not active in the current submitted version. If a future version activates advertising, analytics, or another online service, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated to describe the applicable data practices.

Tracking

The current version does not request permission to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies and does not use App data for targeted advertising.

Device Permissions

The current version does not require access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or precise location. Photo access is requested only for the save feature described above.

Data Retention and Deletion

Game information stored locally remains on your device until you reset it through an available app feature or delete the App. Deleting the App removes its locally stored data, except information that may remain in device backups or system-managed storage.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect names, email addresses, or other direct contact information from children through an account or server because the App does not provide an account system or a Colorflow Colony cloud service.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, third-party services, or data practices change. Any updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
