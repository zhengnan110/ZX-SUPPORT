Colorflow Colony Support

Thank you for playing Colorflow Colony. This page provides gameplay help, troubleshooting information, and contact details for the app.

How to Play

  1. Select an unlocked level from the Map.
  2. Study the colors visible around the outside of the pixel picture.
  3. Tap an exposed color to release its matching grains.
  4. Choose colors in a safe order so the grains can flow into the colony.
  5. Clear the picture to complete the level and unlock its collectible artwork.

Troubleshooting

If a color cannot be cleared: check whether that color is exposed on the outer edge of the picture. Another color may need to be cleared first.

If the app does not respond: close and reopen the app. If the problem continues, restart your iPhone and make sure iOS is up to date.

If progress appears missing: game progress is stored locally on your device. Deleting or reinstalling the app may remove local progress.

When contacting support: include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, level number, and a short description of the issue. A screenshot or screen recording is helpful when available.

Saving Artwork

Colorflow Colony may request permission to add an image to Photos when you choose to save completed artwork, a bead-art preview, or a numbered pattern. If saving fails, check the app's Photos permission in iOS Settings. Denying this permission does not prevent normal gameplay.
