# Water Sort Puzzle Challenge – Support

Welcome to the support page for **Water Sort Puzzle Challenge**, a relaxing color‑sorting puzzle about pouring liquids between tubes.

---

## 🎮 What is the game?
Each tube contains several colored liquid segments. **Pour** from one tube to another and **group same‑color liquid into a single tube** to clear the level.

---

## 📖 How to Play
1. **Tap a source tube**, then **tap a target tube** to pour.
2. You can only pour onto the **same color** or into an **empty** tube.
3. A level is cleared when **every tube contains a single color** (or is empty).

---

## 🧠 Features
- **100+ handcrafted levels** with gradually increasing difficulty  
- **Earn coins** after each win; spend coins in the **Shop** to buy **power‑ups**  
- **Power‑ups:** *Hint*, *Undo*, *Extra Tube*  
- **Smooth animations** and satisfying pour effects  
- **Offline play**—perfect for quick sessions

---

## ❓ FAQ

### Q1: I’m stuck on a level—what can I do?
Use **Hint** to get a suggestion, **Undo** to revert a move, or buy an **Extra Tube** to open up space.

### Q2: Will I lose progress if I reinstall?
Game progress is stored on your device. Deleting the app may remove local data. If you change devices, progress does not transfer unless you use system backups.

### Q3: Does the game require the internet?
**No.** Core gameplay works offline. Only store transactions or future online features may need connectivity.

### Q4: Are there in‑app purchases?
Yes (if enabled in your region/build). You can purchase coin packs or power‑ups from the Shop.

### Q5: The game crashed or behaves strangely—how to report?
Please email us with your device model, iOS version, and a brief description of the steps to reproduce.

---

## 🛠 Tips
- Prioritize creating **empty tubes**—they’re the key to making space.  
- Try to **stack tall runs** of the same color to minimize moves.  
- Use **Undo** liberally to explore without penalty.
