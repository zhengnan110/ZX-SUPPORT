# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 09, 2025
**App Name:** Water Sort Puzzle Challenge

---

## 🔐 Overview
We value your privacy. **Water Sort Puzzle Challenge** is designed to be simple and privacy‑friendly. By default, the app **does not collect**, **store**, or **transmit** personal data to us or to any third‑party server.

---

## 📱 Gameplay & Data Storage
- Gameplay state (e.g., unlocked levels, coins, settings) is stored **locally on your device** in the app’s sandbox.
- No account or sign‑in is required.
- The app can be played **offline**; an internet connection is not required for core gameplay.

---

## 🧰 Purchases & Payments
- If the app offers **in‑app purchases (IAP)** (e.g., coin packs or power‑ups), the purchase transaction is processed by **Apple**. We do not receive your payment information.
- Apple may provide non‑personal, aggregate reports (e.g., sales, downloads) for analytics within App Store Connect.

---

## 🧩 Crash Logs & Diagnostics
- If you opt in to **share analytics with developers** in iOS settings, Apple may provide us **anonymous, aggregate** crash or performance data. This information does not identify you and is used only to improve stability and performance.

---

## 🔗 Third‑Party Services
- **By default, no third‑party SDKs** (analytics/ads) are integrated.
- If we introduce services such as **advertising** or **analytics** in a future update, we will:
  1) update this policy,
  2) request any required permissions/consent in‑app, and
  3) provide links to the third party’s privacy policy.

---

## 👶 Children’s Privacy
The app is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
