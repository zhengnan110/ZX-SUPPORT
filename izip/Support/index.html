Magic Zip Support

Magic Zip is an on-device archive browser and selective extractor for iPhone and iPad. You can inspect an archive before extracting it, choose only the files or folders you need, preview supported media, and share extracted results.

Getting Started

  1. Open Magic Zip and tap Import Archive.
  2. Choose an archive from the iOS Files picker. You can also send an archive to Magic Zip from another app using the Share sheet.
  3. Browse the archive, open folders, and select the files or folders you want.
  4. Tap Extract, then open or share the extracted results.

Magic Zip supports ZIP, ZIPX, CBZ, 7z, RAR4, RAR5, CBR, TAR, TAR.GZ, TGZ, GZ, CAB, ISO, DMG, IMG4, IM4P, IM4M, and IM4R. Some variants, encryption methods, and media previews depend on the archive and the codecs available on your version of iOS.

Extraction Access and Rewarded Ads

Your first extraction task each local day is free. After that, you may choose to watch one Rewarded ad to unlock unlimited extraction for the rest of that local day. Ads never play automatically. Browsing archives and previewing files do not require an ad.

Troubleshooting

An archive will not import: Confirm that the file has finished downloading in Files and that there is enough free storage on the device. Then try importing it again.

A password is rejected: Re-enter the exact password, including capitalization and symbols. Magic Zip supports password-protected ZIP, 7z, RAR4, and RAR5 archives, but not every proprietary archive variant.

A file will not preview: Preview availability depends on iOS Quick Look and the codecs installed on the device. Try extracting the file and opening it in a compatible app.

Extraction stops or fails: Check available storage, keep Magic Zip in the foreground for large jobs, and retry. Interrupted extraction tasks restart rather than resume.

A Rewarded ad is unavailable: Check your internet connection and any required privacy choices, then try again later. Your daily free extraction remains available offline.

A shared archive does not appear: Wait for the “Save to Magic Zip” extension to finish, close the Share sheet, and then open Magic Zip.

Permissions

Archive features use the iOS Files picker and do not require access to your entire photo library. Magic Zip itself does not require camera access for browsing or extraction. An interactive Rewarded ad may offer an optional camera feature; iOS will ask before allowing access, and you may decline.
