Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Magic Zip (the “App”) handles information when you use the current version of the App.

Files and Information Processed on Your Device

Magic Zip processes archives and extracted files locally on your device. The App does not upload archive contents, extracted files, file names, file paths, passwords, or preview data to the developer or to Google. These files are not used to select or measure advertising.

Files selected through the iOS Files picker are copied into the App's sandbox while access is available. Files sent from another app are temporarily copied through the shared App Group inbox and removed after Magic Zip imports them. Media selected for preview is extracted to a temporary directory and removed when the preview closes or during later cleanup.

Accounts and Sign-In

Magic Zip does not require an account or sign-in. The developer does not operate a user account system or a server that stores your archives.

Local App Data

The App stores archive records, imported source archives, extracted results, settings, and daily extraction-access status locally. The daily access record may include whether the free extraction was used, whether a Rewarded ad earned access, a reward transaction identifier used to prevent duplicate grants, the last reward time, and a coarse result such as earned, dismissed, or unavailable. This record does not contain the identity of an archive or file.

Advertising and Third-Party Services

The main App includes Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Magic Zip uses only user-initiated Rewarded ads. It does not show banner, interstitial, or launch ads, and the Share Extension contains no advertising code.

Depending on your region, consent choices, device settings, and Google's serving configuration, Google may process information such as IP address, approximate location, device identifiers, advertising data, product interactions, performance data, crash data, and other diagnostic data for advertising, analytics, consent management, fraud prevention, and service operation. Google may link some of this data and declares that a device identifier may be used for tracking.

Magic Zip does not request App Tracking Transparency permission in this version and does not directly read or store the IDFA. Where required, UMP presents a privacy message before advertising requests are made. A privacy-options control is available in Settings when UMP requires one, allowing you to review or change applicable choices.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information.

Device Permissions

Magic Zip uses the system Files picker for user-selected archives and does not request access to your entire photo library. The App itself does not need the camera for archive browsing or extraction. An interactive Rewarded ad may offer an optional camera feature; iOS will ask for permission before it can be used. Declining camera access does not prevent local archive browsing or the daily free extraction.

Retention and Deletion

You can delete archive records, imported source archives, and extracted results from within the App. Preview files are temporary. Removing the App through iOS deletes data stored in its app container, subject to normal iOS backup and device-management behavior. Information processed independently by Google is retained and deleted according to Google's policies and your applicable privacy choices.

Children's Privacy

Magic Zip is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through an account or developer-operated server. Advertising and consent behavior is governed by device, region, publisher configuration, and applicable Google policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, third-party services, or data practices change. Any updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
