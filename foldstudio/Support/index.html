Ant Fold Studio Support

Ant Fold Studio is a photo collage, carousel and story-video editor for iPhone and iPad. Create with grid layouts, editable examples, text, frames and on-device person cutouts. No app account is required.

Getting Started

  1. Open Editable Examples on Home and choose Start Practice, or start a project with your own selected photos.
  2. Adjust layouts, backgrounds, text, stickers and drawings. Drafts save locally as you edit.
  3. Choose Export, then Images or Video. Image width can be 720, 1080, 2160 or 3240 pixels, or a custom value from 320 to 3240; height follows the project ratio.
  4. To import from another app, open Share, choose this app and confirm Add. Open Shared Inbox on Home and import a new, independent draft.

Troubleshooting

What does Fold Pro unlock?

Fold Pro is a one-time, non-consumable purchase. It adds 12 templates, making all 20 bundled templates available, and increases each project's limit from 10 to 20 pages. The free version includes 8 templates, editing tools and watermark-free image/video export. The price shown by Apple for your storefront applies.

How do I restore a purchase?

Use the Apple Account that made the purchase. Open the Fold Pro screen from Home or open Settings and choose Restore Purchases. If access does not update, check your connection and try again. For refunds, use Apple's purchase support.

Why is no purchase price available?

A price is shown only when the App Store product is available. Check your connection and Apple Account, then retry. If the issue continues, contact us with your app version and storefront.

Where are shared photos?

Open Shared Inbox on Home. Adding images to the inbox does not automatically import a project. Deleting a pending inbox copy does not delete the original photo or a draft already imported.

How do I back up or delete projects?

Export important work or choose cloud backup to save a snapshot in your private iCloud Drive. Cloud backup requires iCloud sign-in and iCloud Drive. Restoring a snapshot creates a new local copy. Delete drafts on Home and clear export cache in Settings. Uninstalling removes app-local data; delete exported Photos items and iCloud snapshots separately where they are stored.

How do I change language or export defaults?

Open Settings on Home. The app supports English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese and Korean. You can follow your device language or choose one, and set the default image export width.

Photos and Files

The system photo picker lets you select photos to import. Saving an export requests permission to add items to Photos. If saving fails, check the app's Photos permission in iOS Settings and available storage. Music and template files are read when you select them; use media you have permission to use.
