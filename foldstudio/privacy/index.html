Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ant Fold Studio (the "App") handles information when you use the App.

Provider and Scope

Ant Fold Studio is provided by Yongmei Shi. This policy describes the current app's local editing, share extension, optional cloud snapshots, template downloads and purchases.

Local Projects and No App Account

No registration or app account is required. Photos, text, drawings, drafts and editing copies are stored on your device by default. Language and export preferences are stored locally. The main app and its share extension use an App Group to share the on-device inbox and necessary preferences, including language.

Only after you select this app in the system share menu and confirm adding does the extension copy selected images into this local inbox. You can preview, import independent drafts or delete pending copies. Inbox deletion does not delete originals or already imported drafts.

Photos, Files and On-Device Image Processing

The system photo picker supplies only photos you choose. The app keeps local copies for editing and requests add-only Photos access when saving exports. Selected large images are oriented and may be reduced to a maximum long edge of 4096 pixels. Music and template files are read when you select them.

Person cutouts and face detection use Apple Vision on your device. Results support the current creative project; they are not used to identify people or build a face database. Photos and these results are not sent to the developer. Local project data is retained until you delete it or remove the app, subject to any copies or backups you choose to keep.

Advertising, Analytics and Tracking

The current app has no advertising, analytics or tracking SDK and no developer-operated backend. It does not send your photos or drafts to the developer or use them for advertising.

Apple Purchases

Apple StoreKit handles Fold Pro purchases, transaction verification and purchase restoration. The app uses verified transactions to determine purchased access. We do not receive your payment-card information. Apple's handling of its services is governed by Apple's privacy practices.

Optional iCloud Snapshots and Sharing

A project is copied to your private iCloud Drive only when you choose cloud backup. Apple manages iCloud storage and transfer. Restoring a snapshot makes a new local copy without automatically merging with or replacing an existing draft. The developer has no access to your private iCloud snapshots.

When you export or use the system share sheet, files you choose are passed to the destination you select. That app or service handles them under its own privacy practices. Exports already saved to Photos or another destination remain there until you delete them separately.

User-Requested Template Downloads

The app makes a request to a website when you enter an HTTPS address and choose Download. That website can receive normal request information such as your IP address and may retain request logs according to its practices. Downloads are limited to JSON layouts; the app does not upload your photos or drafts with that request. The current version has no developer-operated template-download server.

Support Correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include, and use that correspondence to answer and resolve your request. We retain it only as needed for support or applicable obligations. Avoid sending sensitive personal photos. You can contact us to request deletion of support correspondence.

Retention and Deletion

Delete drafts and pending inbox copies from the app, and clear export cache in Settings. Uninstalling removes app-local data. Resetting preferences does not delete drafts or purchased access. Delete exported files and private iCloud snapshots separately from their respective storage locations. Apple manages purchase records; removing the app does not cancel or erase a permanent purchase.

Children's Privacy

The app does not require a child's account or ask for children's personal information. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through the app. If a child has sent personal information in support correspondence, a parent or guardian can contact us for help with deletion.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, third-party services, or data practices change. Any updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
