Hanzi Coach Support

Thank you for using Hanzi Coach. This page provides help and contact information for the app.

Getting Started

  1. Choose one or more Chinese characters from the Library.
  2. Create an A4 worksheet and print it.
  3. Complete the worksheet by hand.
  4. Open Scoring and photograph or import the completed worksheet.
  5. Review the character scores and available stroke-level feedback.
  6. Save the result if you want to keep it in your practice history.

Worksheet Scoring

Supported worksheets: scoring is designed for A4 worksheets generated by Hanzi Coach. Worksheets from other sources or with different layouts may not be recognized correctly.

For better results: place the worksheet on a flat, well-lit surface, keep the entire page visible, avoid shadows and glare, and hold the camera parallel to the paper.

About scores: results are intended as practice guidance. Camera angle, lighting, paper distortion, writing tools, and stray marks may affect the score.

Troubleshooting

If a worksheet is not recognized: confirm that it was generated by the app and that all page markers and QR codes are clearly visible.

If camera access is unavailable: allow camera access in iOS Settings, or use the photo import option.

If the app does not respond: close and reopen the app. If the problem continues, restart your device and make sure iOS is up to date.

When contacting support: include your device model, iOS version, app version, and a short description of the issue. A screenshot is helpful when available.
